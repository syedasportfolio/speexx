Two honest notes. Not every problem is a messaging problem, so booking, content and audio issues go to the product team, not into nudges. And these reviews skew positive: many were posted in batches, and people who quietly stop learning rarely write reviews. They show what keeps active learners going, not why silent ones leave. Section 04 covers how I'd find that out.

03 · Five messages along the learner journey

A message for every moment learners hesitate

Tap a highlighted step to see the message as a learner would, why it should work, and how I'd measure it. Push notifications follow current guidance: a title under 40 characters and a one-line body, so they read in full even when collapsed. Anything longer goes by email. Each one runs as an A/B test against the current message.

04 · What I'd do in the first 90 days

Data shows where. Interviews show why. Tests prove the fix.

Weeks 1 to 4

Map the drop-off

Use product data to build the learner funnel below, split by client, language and course type, and find the biggest drop.

Weeks 3 to 6

Ask the quiet learners

With Customer Success, interview 15 to 20 learners who stopped at that step. Reviews only show the people who stayed.

Weeks 6 to 12

Test, then roll out

A/B test the messages for the weakest step first. Turn the winners into a playbook Customer Success can use with every client.
